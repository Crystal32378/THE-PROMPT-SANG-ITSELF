What was meant to be an instruction became part of the artwork.

The Prompt

The music-direction prompt written by Niuniu for Music 3.0:

Instrumental neo-classical ambient underscore. Felt piano as the lead voice — sparse, intimate, soft hammer attack, close-miked. Soft detuned string pads underneath, very low and sustained, no vibrato. 70 BPM, no percussion, no drums, no beat. D minor key, slow harmonic movement on D, A, F, G, C only. Quiet, restrained, breathing-room between phrases. Warm, boutique fitting room atmosphere, not a tech demo. Designed to sit underneath a calm female voiceover without competing. Structure: near-silence opening, gradual build of low sustained pad, recede back to near-silence, single soft resolving phrase on D5 that hangs unresolved. Avoid bright major-key lifts, avoid sudden dynamic swells, avoid arpeggios, avoid piano runs. Think: Ryuichi Sakamoto meets Hildur Guðnadóttir meets a couture showroom at 3am. End unresolved-then-soft.

The Lyrics

The structure directions supplied in the lyrics field:

[Intro]
(Sparse felt piano, single note, far away, room reverb)

(Soft attack on a low D, sustained with breath)

(15 seconds of near silence, single high F floats in)

(Long pause, distant harmonics)

[Verse]
(Piano begins a slow, minimal phrase in D minor)

(Gentle, intimate touch, like trying not to wake someone)

(Low string pad fades in underneath, barely there)

(Soft pulse at 70 BPM, almost imperceptible)

(Strings swell, a low sustained D and A underneath)

(Second piano voice enters, simple counter-melody)

[Bridge]
(Piano recedes to single notes, strings fade)

(Long silence, room tone only)

(One last piano phrase, five notes rising to D5)

(D5 sustains and hangs unresolved)

[Outro]
(D5 slowly fades into room reverb)

(Tail of reverb decays into silence)

What Happened

The prompt asked for unresolved-then-soft. The lyrics field contained structure directions. The generated output rendered the first line as a vocal rather than as a non-vocal direction.

The instruction became the lyric.
